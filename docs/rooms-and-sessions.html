Rooms and sessions

A Room is the view you're working in together right now. A session is the view you keep. This document is for planning what people can do as they move between the two.

Reflects the code on October 31, 2026. Decision recorded in ADR 0004.

Room: live

A running object on the server. It takes changes from the model and from people's hands, puts them in order, and keeps every Viewer in step.

It's built for the conversation and the meeting. Its contents vanish after a day without use.

Session: at rest

A JSON document that never changes once it's written. It can sit in a file or be compressed into a URL, and it can include IGV and Juicebox.

It's built for keeping and sharing. It has been the workhorse since long before the MCP server.

The Room doesn't replace the session. Each one covers what the other can't: a session can't be edited live by a group, and a Room can't last. So every interesting experience comes down to when and how a view crosses from one to the other.

What a Room is

A Room is one running instance of the Room class (server/src/room.ts), a Cloudflare Durable Object. There's one for each Room id. It has no visual part: the Viewer is the web page that draws, and the Room only tells it what to draw.

It holdsThe viewer state (ensemble URL and place in it, render style, structure appearance, viewer settings, camera), its version, what Viewers have reported drawing, and news for the model.
It doesn't holdThe 3D data, a history of earlier versions, the conversation, or who anyone is. The Room id is effectively the key to the Room.
It accepts changes fromThe model's tool calls and people's hand changes, as equal proposals. The Room alone applies them.
Its lifetimeIt expires after a day without use, and its contents are gone for good. Opening the same id again starts it empty.

What a session is

A session is JSON with up to three parts, one per viewer. Spacewalk writes the first part and treats the other two as IGV's and Juicebox's own business:

PartWritten byWhat it coversCan a Room use it?
spacewalkSpacewalkEnsemble, locus, trace and group, render style, structure appearance, camera, checked tracks, open panelsThe 3D parts
igvIGV (browser.toJSON())IGV's own session: tracks and their setupNo
juiceboxJuicebox (hic.toJSON())Juicebox's own session, when a Hi-C map is loadedNo

Viewer settings (background, axes, ground plane, ruler) are never in a session. The full app keeps them in the browser's localStorage, and a Room keeps them in its viewer state. A snapshot URL carries them in a parameter of their own, beside the session (ADR 0002).

The same content takes three forms

A session holds URLs and settings, not the data itself, so any session only works for as long as the ensemble's URL does. Short links also depend on the shortener staying up.

They agree on the 3D scene and differ everywhere else, and those differences decide what survives a crossing.

Moving between them today

Building a Room from a session

You give the model a shareable URL or session string, and it calls load_session. A short link is followed to the full URL. The Spacewalk part becomes one change that replaces everything the Room shows, for everyone in it. The model's reply names any dropped IGV or Hi-C content.

Saving a Room as a session

You ask the model for a link, and it calls get_shareable_url. The Room writes a reduced Spacewalk part and the viewer settings into a snapshot URL. The Room carries on, and the link stays frozen at that moment. Only the model can do this, and there's no button in the Viewer.

A meeting in a Room

Here's the scenario this document is mainly for. A Driver opens a Room from a session someone saved last week, and shares the Room link. Guests join, and the group explores with the model and with their own hands. The meeting ends, and someone asks: what do we want to keep?

Shared Rooms are planned for milestone M3, which hasn't started. A second Viewer already gets every change today, but Guests, live camera following and multi-Viewer reporting are still to come.

What would actually happen today. A Guest has no way to save, because saving goes through the Driver's conversation. The Driver has to remember to ask, and must do it before the Room's contents expire. The result opens only on the Viewer page, as a link. If Spacewalk runs inside geenee, the milestone plan also withholds get_shareable_url, which would remove the only way out of the Room.

What might "save" mean?

People may want different things when they ask to keep a meeting. These are the things we could design for. Only the first has any support today.

The final view

Where the group ended up. Supported today through a snapshot URL, when someone asks the model.

The moments along the way

"Keep this one." The model can make several snapshot links during a meeting, but nothing prompts it, and nothing gathers them.

A file to archive

A .json a lab can store with its data, like the ones it already has. It isn't possible from a Room.

A way back to the full app

Continue the work with IGV and Juicebox beside the 3D view. It needs a session the full app will accept.

The reasoning

Why this view mattered. That lives in the conversation, and a session has no place for it. A summary could sit next to the links it describes.

The Room itself

Pick up tomorrow in the same Room link. A saved session can seed a new live view, but the same id starts empty after expiry and has no memory of it.

Questions to work through

None of these are decided. The sketches under each are starting points for discussion.

Who can save?

Guests are equal to the Driver in what the Viewers show, yet only the Driver's model can save. Should anyone in the Room be able to?

A "Copy link to this view" control in the Viewer would give everyone the same power. M3 leaves roles out of scope, so limiting who can save would need new concepts.

Do we ask the model, press a button, or both?

Asking fits the conversation, but the model has to be involved and Guests can't reach it. A button needs no model, though the model wouldn't know about the save unless it was told.

A hand save could appear in the model's news, as hand changes already do, so it can mention what was kept.

How do we protect the end of the meeting?

Nothing prompts a save, and an expired Room can't be recovered. It also has no identity to contact, or anywhere of its own to send a last save.

The model could offer a link when the conversation seems to wind down. The Viewer could show how long until expiry. Keeping an automatic final save would raise the question of who owns it.

Should a Room remember moments?

Today's single-version record keeps the Room simple, but it means the path through a meeting disappears.

Named bookmarks in the Room, kept as session strings, could become a list at the end. A full version history would be much larger and would raise harder questions, such as whether undo applies to everyone.

How does a file come out of a Room?

People trust the session file, but a remote server can't write to their disk, and a tool reply isn't a download.

A save button on the Viewer page could download the file, though that page currently leaves saving to the full app. Another route is a link that opens in the full app, which can then save.

Should "Open in full Spacewalk" exist?

It would turn a meeting's result into the app people already know, with IGV and Juicebox available. It needs a block the full app accepts, which means tolerating missing panels and locus or writing them.

Loading a Room's block could be made tolerant in the full app. A locus would have to come from the ensemble, which is something only a Viewer has loaded.

Should a Room carry what it doesn't understand?

Loading a full-app session into a Room and saving it loses the IGV and Juicebox parts, even though nobody in the Room changed them.

A Room could keep those parts untouched and write them back on save. ADR 0001 rejected an opaque session as the Room's record, but it didn't rule out carrying parts that the Room never reads. The risk is that the 3D view and the saved IGV locus drift apart.

What should loading into a busy Room do?

Loading replaces the view for everyone at once, with no way back. In a meeting, that can erase what the group was just looking at.

The model could offer to save first, or a load could open a new Room instead. The tool description could also tell it to check whether Viewers are connected.

What's the right save path inside geenee?

The M2 plan withholds get_shareable_url, as it does for IGV. That leaves a Spacewalk Room in geenee with no way to save.

Geenee could offer a Workspace-level save covering all three viewers. That could also be the natural home for a real combined session.

Rough edges found while writing this

  • Silent drops: load_session doesn't mention dropped locus or panel state.
  • A false IGV warning: the full app writes sessionURL into every Share URL, so from reading the code, load_session reports IGV tracks as not loaded even when there were none.
  • Silent failure in the full app: a block with no panelVisibility makes loadSession return with nothing loaded and no message. Anything that hands the full app a Room's block will meet this first.
  • Two definitions of one shape: structure appearance is read field by field in both repos, so a new field must be added to each.
